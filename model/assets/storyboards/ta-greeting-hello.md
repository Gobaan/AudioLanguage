## Beat 1: Setup / Cue

Dialogue line: Friend says, "Hello!"

Video prompt:

3-5 second realistic video. A learner sits at a small desk studying, with an open notebook, pencil, and headphones beside the notebook. A friend walks in through an open doorway, slows down when they see the learner, smiles, and gives a friendly wave while saying hello. The learner looks up from the notebook, surprised but happy. Stable medium shot with both characters visible, warm indoor light, casual home study room, natural body language, no subtitles, no dialogue text, no translations, no logos.

Learning purpose:

The friend's entrance, smile, eye contact, and wave make the greeting visually obvious before the learner understands the sound.

Gesture notes:

- Wave maps to "Hello."
- Friend's gaze toward the learner shows who is being addressed.
- Learner looking up marks that the learner is now expected to respond.

Gesture notes:

- Wave maps to "Hello."
- Open-hand gesture toward friend maps to "you."
- Raised eyebrows and slight lean map to asking a question.

## Beat 3: Response / Resolution

Dialogue line: Friend says, "Fine. Thank you."

Video prompt:

3-5 second realistic video, same study room, same characters, same clothes, same warm lighting. Camera switches to the friend's face and upper body. The friend smiles warmly, nods once to show they are fine, then lightly touches their chest and gives a small appreciative nod for thank you. The learner is visible in the foreground, relaxed and pleased that the exchange went well. Stable medium close-up, ordinary friendly mood, no subtitles, no dialogue text, no translations, no logos.

Learning purpose:

The friend's relaxed face and nod show a positive response. The hand-to-chest and appreciative nod support the feeling of "thank you" without writing or translating it.

Gesture notes:
