Yes, 5 scenarios is enough for an MVP test, but only if we’re clear about what it can prove.

Right now you are not testing “can this teach Japanese.” You are testing a smaller, sharper thing:

Does visual-context + audio + spoken response feel learnable enough that a beginner wants to keep going?

With 5 cards, you can test:

Can users understand what the scene wants without translation?
Do the comic frames make the learner’s turn obvious?
Does hearing the full mini-dialogue first help?
Can users repeat or produce the target line after one exposure?
Does AI feedback feel fair enough, or frustrating/random?
Is the flow fun enough to do 5 cards without quitting?
Do users remember any phrases 10 minutes later or the next day?
What 5 cards cannot test well:

Long-term retention
Spaced repetition
Whether this scales to 2,000 words
Whether users can transfer phrases to many new contexts
Whether people will pay
Whether AI judging stays reliable across many languages
The most important MVP test is this:

Give a beginner 5 visual dialogue cards.
Do not show translations up front.
Let them watch, then speak in context.
After the session, show the same situations again with slightly different visuals or order.
Ask: can they choose/say the right phrase from the scene alone?
The next day, test the same 5 again with one or two transfer scenes.
Your success signals:

They can complete 4/5 cards without needing text help.
They can explain the rough meaning from the scene, not from English translation.
They can recall at least 2-3 phrases after a delay.
They say “I get what I’m supposed to do” instead of “I’m guessing.”
The AI judge does not feel like the main obstacle.
The biggest risk to test first is not content volume. It’s whether the learning loop feels fair: “I saw the situation, I heard the phrase, I tried it, the app understood me, and I improved.”

So yes: 5 scenarios is enough for the first test. The next useful step is probably making those 5 polished and consistent, then testing with 3-5 real beginners before adding more content
